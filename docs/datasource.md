## Foundations, Surveys & Threat Model

- [ ] **[Post-Quantum Cryptography (textbook)](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-540-88702-7)** — *Book, 2009* (Springer; eds. Bernstein, Buchmann, Dahmen). The canonical edited PQC textbook with foundational chapters across all families plus the seminal "Introduction to post-quantum cryptography"; the most-cited reference work for framing a PQC paper.
- [ ] **[Polynomial-Time Algorithms for Prime Factorization and Discrete Logarithms on a Quantum Computer](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9508027)** — *Paper, 1996* (Peter W. Shor). Shor's seminal paper giving efficient quantum algorithms for factoring and discrete logs — the result that breaks RSA/ECC and motivates the entire PQC field.
- [ ] **[A fast quantum mechanical algorithm for database search](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9605043)** — *Paper, 1996* (Lov K. Grover). Grover's O(sqrt(N)) quantum search giving a quadratic speedup, the basis for quantum attacks on symmetric primitives and the rationale for doubling key/hash sizes.
- [ ] **[Post-quantum cryptography (Nature review)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature23461)** — *Survey/SoK, 2017* (Bernstein & Lange, Nature 549:188-194). Authoritative Nature review framing PQC: why current public-key systems break under quantum computers and how post-quantum systems are built.
- [ ] **[Post-quantum cryptography---dealing with the fallout of physics success](https://eprint.iacr.org/2017/314)** — *Paper, 2017* (Bernstein & Lange; IACR ePrint). Overview of the central PQC challenge of meeting usability and flexibility demands without sacrificing trust; pairs with the Nature article as a freely accessible introduction.
- [ ] **[Post-Quantum Cryptography and Quantum-Safe Security: A Comprehensive Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10436)** — *Survey/SoK, 2026* (Chhetri et al.; arXiv cs.CR). Comprehensive survey mapping PQC from foundations to practice across all families, covering assumptions, performance, hardware, side channels, integration, and migration; a strong single-source backbone.
- [ ] **[Envisioning the Future of Cyber Security in Post-Quantum Era: A Survey on PQ Standardization, Applications, Challenges and Opportunities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.12037)** — *Survey/SoK, 2023* (Darzi et al.; arXiv cs.CR). Broad PQC survey covering design, structural vulnerabilities, side-channel-focused implementation evaluation, standardization, and beyond-standardization schemes.
- [ ] **[A Survey on Security with Quantum Computing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00058)** — *Survey/SoK, 2026* (Sangala et al.; arXiv cs.CR). Comprehensive review of quantum-computing security including the threat to classical cryptographic infrastructure and quantum-resilient solutions; wider context for the quantum threat landscape.
- [ ] **[Quantum Disruption: An SoK of How Post-Quantum Attackers Reshape Blockchain Security and Performance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.13333)** — *Survey/SoK, 2025* (Mallick et al.; arXiv cs.CR). SoK analyzing how PQC migration affects blockchain primitives, performance, and trust, demonstrating that PQC is not a drop-in replacement.
- [ ] **[Study of Post-Quantum status of Widely Used Protocols](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28728)** — *Survey/SoK, 2026* (Mallick et al., Cisco Research; arXiv cs.NI). Survey of quantum vulnerability and PQC-migration progress across nine deployed protocols, finding key exchange easier to migrate than authentication.
- [ ] **[Using Learning with Rounding to Instantiate Post-Quantum Cryptographic Algorithms (SoK)](https://eprint.iacr.org/2025/1382)** — *Survey/SoK, 2025* (Basso, Bos, D'Anvers, Karmakar et al.; IACR ePrint). Focused SoK of Learning-with-Rounding-based PQC: theoretical foundations, algorithmic developments, and practical implementations. _(link unverified — confirm before citing)_
- [ ] **[On Lattices, Learning with Errors, Random Linear Codes, and Cryptography](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.03703)** — *Paper, 2024* (Oded Regev; arXiv author version). Regev's foundational paper introducing the LWE problem and a quantum worst-case-to-average-case reduction; the bedrock hardness assumption for nearly all lattice PQC.
- [ ] **[On Ideal Lattices and Learning with Errors Over Rings (Ring-LWE)](https://eprint.iacr.org/2012/230)** — *Paper, 2012* (Lyubashevsky, Peikert, Regev; IACR ePrint). Introduces Ring-LWE and proves its hardness from worst-case ideal-lattice problems, enabling efficient structured lattice cryptography.
- [ ] **[Worst-Case to Average-Case Reductions for Module Lattices (Module-LWE / Module-SIS)](https://eprint.iacr.org/2012/090)** — *Paper, 2012* (Langlois, Stehle; IACR ePrint). Defines Module-LWE and Module-SIS with worst-case-to-average-case reductions; the hardness foundation for Kyber, Dilithium, and Saber.
- [ ] **[Fiat-Shamir with Aborts: Applications to Lattice and Factoring-Based Signatures](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/asiacrypt/Lyubashevsky09)** — *Paper, 2009* (Lyubashevsky, ASIACRYPT 2009). The original Fiat-Shamir with Aborts paradigm using rejection sampling, the core technique behind Dilithium and most lattice Fiat-Shamir signatures.
- [ ] **[Trapdoors for Hard Lattices and New Cryptographic Constructions (GPV)](https://eprint.iacr.org/2007/432)** — *Paper, 2007* (Gentry, Peikert, Vaikuntanathan; IACR ePrint). The GPV framework introducing preimage-sampleable trapdoor functions and hash-and-sign lattice signatures; the theoretical basis for Falcon.
- [ ] **[Publicly Verifiable Zero-Knowledge and Post-Quantum Signatures From VOLE-in-the-Head (FAEST)](https://eprint.iacr.org/2023/996)** — *Paper, 2023* (Baum et al., CRYPTO 2023; IACR ePrint). Introduces VOLE-in-the-Head, the technique underlying the FAEST signature; core reference for VOLEitH/MPCitH signatures.
- [ ] **[One Tree to Rule Them All: Optimizing GGM Trees and OWFs for Post-Quantum Signatures](https://eprint.iacr.org/2024/490)** — *Paper, 2024* (Baum et al., ASIACRYPT 2024; IACR ePrint). Fundamental optimization of GGM-tree-based MPC-in-the-Head/VOLEitH signatures, improving signing performance for schemes like FAEST.

## Lattice-based Schemes

- [ ] **[CRYSTALS -- Kyber: a CCA-secure module-lattice-based KEM](https://eprint.iacr.org/2017/634)** — *Paper, 2017* (Bos et al.; IACR ePrint). The original Kyber paper introducing the Module-LWE-based CCA-secure KEM that became the basis for the NIST ML-KEM standard.
- [ ] **[CRYSTALS -- Dilithium: Digital Signatures from Module Lattices](https://eprint.iacr.org/2017/633)** — *Paper, 2017* (Ducas et al.; IACR ePrint). The original Dilithium signature paper, based on Module-LWE and Module-SIS using Fiat-Shamir with Aborts; the basis for the NIST ML-DSA standard.
- [ ] **[CRYSTALS official project website (Kyber and Dilithium)](https://pq-crystals.org/)** — *Portal/website* (pq-crystals.org). Canonical home for CRYSTALS-Kyber and CRYSTALS-Dilithium, hosting round-3 specifications, software, and resources.
- [ ] **[CRYSTALS-Dilithium Algorithm Specifications and Supporting Documentation (Round 3)](https://pq-crystals.org/dilithium/data/dilithium-specification-round3-20210208.pdf)** — *Spec/RFC, 2021* (pq-crystals.org). Official round-3 Dilithium NIST submission specification with full parameter sets and design rationale; authoritative spec preceding FIPS 204.
- [ ] **[Falcon: Fast-Fourier Lattice-based Compact Signatures over NTRU (official specification)](https://falcon-sign.info/falcon.pdf)** — *Spec/RFC, 2020* (Falcon project). Official Falcon NIST submission specification, an NTRU-lattice hash-and-sign scheme on the GPV framework with fast Fourier sampling; smallest signatures among lattice finalists.
- [ ] **[Falcon official project website](https://falcon-sign.info/)** — *Portal/website* (Falcon design team). Landing page hosting the specification, reference and optimized implementations, and supporting documentation; canonical project source for Falcon.
- [ ] **[Saber: Module-LWR based key exchange, CPA-secure encryption and CCA-secure KEM](https://eprint.iacr.org/2018/230)** — *Paper, 2018* (D'Anvers et al.; IACR ePrint). The original Saber paper introducing a Module-LWR-based KEM with power-of-two moduli avoiding rejection sampling; a NIST PQC finalist.
- [ ] **[Post-quantum key exchange -- a new hope](https://eprint.iacr.org/2015/1092)** — *Paper, 2015* (Alkim, Ducas, Poppelmann, Schwabe; IACR ePrint). The seminal NewHope Ring-LWE key exchange paper that demonstrated practical post-quantum key exchange and directly influenced Kyber's design.
- [ ] **[NewHope without reconciliation (NewHope-Simple)](https://eprint.iacr.org/2016/1157)** — *Paper, 2016* (Alkim, Ducas, Poppelmann, Schwabe; IACR ePrint). Introduces NewHope-Simple, replacing error reconciliation with a straightforward Ring-LWE-to-KEM transform; relevant to the NewHope NIST submission lineage.
- [ ] **[Frodo: Take off the ring! Practical, Quantum-Secure Key Exchange from LWE](https://eprint.iacr.org/2016/659)** — *Paper, 2016* (Bos et al.; IACR ePrint). The original Frodo paper presenting practical key exchange from plain (unstructured) LWE, the foundation of the conservative FrodoKEM submission.
- [ ] **[FrodoKEM: A CCA-Secure Learning With Errors Key Encapsulation Mechanism](https://eprint.iacr.org/2025/1861)** — *Paper, 2025* (Glabush et al.; IACR ePrint). Up-to-date specification-style paper for FrodoKEM, the conservative plain-LWE KEM now standardized by ISO and recommended by BSI.
- [ ] **[FrodoKEM official project website](https://frodokem.org/)** — *Portal/website* (FrodoKEM design team). Canonical FrodoKEM site hosting specifications (including the ISO standardization proposal), implementations, and documentation for the plain-LWE KEM family.
- [ ] **[NTRU: A Ring-Based Public Key Cryptosystem](https://doi.org/10.1007/BFb0054868)** — *Paper, 1998* (Hoffstein, Pipher, Silverman; Springer ANTS-III). The original NTRU paper introducing the ring-based cryptosystem whose lattice problem underlies the NTRU and Falcon families.
- [ ] **[NTRU official project website (NIST submission)](https://ntru.org/)** — *Portal/website* (NTRU NIST submission). Canonical site for the round-3 NTRU KEM submission, hosting specifications, talks, and reference implementations. _(link unverified — confirm before citing)_
- [ ] **[NTRU Prime official project website](https://ntruprime.cr.yp.to/)** — *Portal/website* (NTRU Prime project; Bernstein et al.). Canonical NTRU Prime site hosting the specification, security estimates, and round-3 documentation for the reduced-attack-surface NTRU-style KEM.

## Hash-based Schemes

- [ ] **[The SPHINCS+ Signature Framework](https://eprint.iacr.org/2019/1086)** — *Paper, 2019* (Bernstein et al., ACM CCS 2019; IACR ePrint). The foundational academic paper behind SLH-DSA/SPHINCS+, introducing FORS and tweakable hash functions for a unified security analysis.
- [ ] **[SPHINCS+ Official Project Website](https://sphincs.org/)** — *Portal/website* (SPHINCS+ design team). Canonical project site with specifications (incl. v3.1), reference software, and resources; useful for parameter sets and reference implementations.
- [ ] **[RFC 8391: XMSS — eXtended Merkle Signature Scheme](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc8391)** — *Spec/RFC, 2018* (IRTF/IETF). Official specification of the stateful hash-based scheme XMSS (and XMSS^MT and WOTS+); primary reference for stateful hash-based signatures.
- [ ] **[RFC 8554: Leighton-Micali Hash-Based Signatures (LMS/HSS)](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8554.html)** — *Spec/RFC, 2019* (IRTF/IETF; McGrew, Curcio, Fluhrer). Official specification of the LMS stateful hash-based signature system and the hierarchical HSS scheme built atop it; companion to XMSS.

## Isogeny-based & Multivariate & Other Families

- [ ] **[Towards Quantum-Resistant Cryptosystems from Supersingular Elliptic Curve Isogenies](https://eprint.iacr.org/2011/506)** — *Paper, 2011* (Jao, De Feo, PQCrypto 2011; IACR ePrint). The seminal SIDH paper that launched isogeny-based key exchange and underpinned SIKE; essential historical reference for the isogeny family.
- [ ] **[CSIDH: An Efficient Post-Quantum Commutative Group Action](https://eprint.iacr.org/2018/383)** — *Paper, 2018* (Castryck et al., ASIACRYPT 2018; IACR ePrint). - [ ] **[SQIsign: Compact Post-Quantum Signatures from Quaternions and Isogenies](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/1240)** — *Paper, 2020* (De Feo et al., ASIACRYPT 2020; IACR ePrint). - [ ] **[UOV: Unbalanced Oil and Vinegar — NIST Specification](https://csrc.nist.gov/csrc/media/Projects/pqc-dig-sig/documents/round-2/spec-files/uov-spec-round2-web.pdf)** — *Spec/RFC, 2024* (NIST CSRC). Official Round-2 specification of UOV, a third-round additional-signatures candidate and the canonical surviving multivariate scheme.
- [ ] **[MAYO: Practical Post-Quantum Signatures from Oil-and-Vinegar Maps](https://eprint.iacr.org/2021/1144)** — *Paper, 2021* (Ward Beullens, SAC 2021; IACR ePrint). The originating paper for MAYO, a UOV variant with much smaller public keys via "whipping"; a NIST third-round additional-signature candidate.
- [ ] **[GeMSS: A Great Multivariate Short Signature (NIST Specification)](https://www-polsys.lip6.fr/Links/NIST/GeMSS_specification.pdf)** — *Spec/RFC, 2018* (PolSys / LIP6). Official specification of GeMSS, the HFEv--based multivariate signature with very short signatures but large keys; reference for the big-field multivariate signature line. _(link unverified — confirm before citing)_

State-of-the-art power-analysis key recovery on Falcon's Gaussian samplers plus a practical countermeasure for the FN-DSA implementation.
- [ ] **[Forging Dilithium and Falcon Signatures by Single Fault Injection](https://eprint.iacr.org/2025/2029)** — *Paper, 2025* (Bauer, De Santis; IACR ePrint). Single-fault-injection signature forgery against ML-DSA (Dilithium) and FN-DSA (Falcon); relevant to embedded boot/firmware-verification threat models.
- [ ] **[A Single Trace Fault Injection Attack on Hedged CRYSTALS-Dilithium](https://eprint.iacr.org/2024/238)** — *Paper, 2024* (Jendral; IACR ePrint). Voltage-glitching fault attack on hedged-mode CRYSTALS-Dilithium on Cortex-M4, showing hedged/randomized signing does not prevent fault key recovery.
- [ ] **[Practical Fault Injection Attacks on SPHINCS](https://eprint.iacr.org/2018/674)** — *Paper, 2018* (Genet et al.; IACR ePrint). First practical fault attack on hash-based signatures, mounting a universal forgery on SPHINCS via voltage glitching and discussing XMSS, LMS, and SPHINCS+.
- [ ] **[Faulting Winternitz One-Time Signatures to forge LMS, XMSS, or SPHINCS+ signatures](https://eprint.iacr.org/2023/1572)** — *Paper, 2023* (Wagner et al.; IACR ePrint). Fault-injection attack on the WOTS building block common to all standardized hash-based signature schemes (LMS, XMSS, SPHINCS+/SLH-DSA).
- [ ] **[Exploiting Small-Norm Polynomial Multiplication with Physical Attacks: Application to CRYSTALS-Dilithium](https://eprint.iacr.org/2023/1545)** — *Paper, 2023* (Bronchain et al.; IACR ePrint). Profiled physical (power/EM) attacks accumulating noisy secret-key information across many Dilithium signatures and exploiting it via belief propagation for full key recovery.
- [ ] **[Formal verification of the PQXDH Post-Quantum key agreement protocol (USENIX Security 2024)](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/bhargavan)** — *Paper, 2024* (Bhargavan et al., USENIX Security). Peer-reviewed paper formally analyzing Signal's PQXDH; the rigorous academic complement to the deployed Signal spec.
- [ ] **[Protecting Dilithium against Leakage: Revisited Sensitivity Analysis and Improved Implementations](https://eprint.iacr.org/2022/1406)** — *Paper, 2022* (Azouaoui et al.; IACR ePrint). Refined side-channel sensitivity analysis and improved masking gadgets/conversions for CRYSTALS-Dilithium; a key reference for masking countermeasures on ML-DSA.
- [ ] **[Finding and Protecting the Weakest Link - On Side-Channel Attacks on y in Masked ML-DSA](https://eprint.iacr.org/2025/276)** — *Paper, 2025* (Hermelink, Ning, Petri; IACR ePrint). Analyzes side-channel leakage of the masking nonce y in fully masked ML-DSA and proposes protections; addresses a gap in SCA understanding for protected ML-DSA.
- [ ] **[High-order Polynomial Comparison and Masking Lattice-based Encryption](https://eprint.iacr.org/2021/1615)** — *Paper, 2021* (Coron et al.; IACR ePrint). Provides and proves (in the ISW probing model) high-order polynomial-comparison techniques needed to mask the decryption of IND-CCA lattice KEMs such as Kyber.
- [ ] **[Improved ML-DSA Hardware Implementation With First Order Masking Countermeasure](https://eprint.iacr.org/2024/1817)** — *Paper, 2024* (Raj et al.; IACR ePrint). First-order masked hardware implementation of ML-DSA, porting Dilithium software masking gadgets to hardware; relevant to hardware masking countermeasure cost.
